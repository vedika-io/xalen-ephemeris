# 12-month public reference and reproducibility programme

We request up to INR 1,500,000 over twelve months for maintenance of an existing Indian-sponsored open computation project. Work and expenditure are conditional on an agreed award. A smaller, genuinely narrower first phase can be scoped if appropriate.

Public value: numerical software is difficult to trust or integrate when its time scales, frames, reference sources and data dependencies are implicit. Xalen Ephemeris already has typed time representations, coordinate routines, VSOP87 and DE440/SPK provider source, official-reference test designs and a seeded comparison harness. We propose a public reference and reproducibility kit that lets another developer trace a result, identify a discrepancy and contribute a fix. Funded outputs will be reusable without a commercial account.

Differentiation: the proposed work connects a shared Rust computation core to an initial existing binding and documents astronomical and cultural-calendar conventions. It complements established tools rather than asserting superior accuracy or novel orbital science. Applicable upstream notices are preserved. Some current kernel tests can skip missing data and some vectors are self-consistency checks: the funded corpus will label those separately from independent references and report skips explicitly.

Scope: xalen-time, xalen-coords and xalen-ephem, plus one initial existing binding chosen and documented at the baseline milestone. Apache-2.0 computation code with applicable BSD-3-Clause notices only. Proprietary Vedika/GrahaGuru services and the separately restricted optional catalogue are excluded. No commercial inference or company-wide expansion is funded by this request.

Proposed public milestones from award start:
Months 1-3: publish a versioned source/dependency/licence inventory for the three focus crates and selected binding; document a computation-only build path and its actual results; publish a reference schema recording source, time scale, frame, centre, units, tolerance and oracle class. Record the initial issue/release baseline and limitations.
Months 4-6: assemble at least 100 provenance-checked fixtures, including at least 10 independently sourced DE440/Horizons vectors and 25 boundary/failure cases. At least 24 cases will receive manual provenance review. Existing cases count only after that review. Publish generators and actual run results, including deviations, failures, skipped checks and environment/backend settings. These numbers are future verification targets, not achieved results.
Months 7-9: document and verify the chosen binding build/smoke path on two supported platform configurations. Commission separately attributable numerical/reference and licence/data-boundary reviews, with reviewers appointed only after funding. Publish findings and a finding-to-fix/test map; retain unresolved findings visibly. Complete independently reviewed build/binding/contributor documentation.
Months 10-12: publish release-readiness and provenance checklists, a scoped release candidate only if relevant gates pass, a measured outcome report against baseline and the next maintenance backlog. Provide four substantive public community updates across the year. Registry publication is reported only if completed and verified.

Proposed budget, subject to founder/funder agreement: INR 960,000 maintainer project time (planning basis INR 80,000/month for 12 months and 60 planned hours/month, up to 720 hours annually; no full-time OSS role claimed). Payment structure and capacity remain subject to the grant agreement and milestone acceptance. INR 240,000 for independent numerical/reference/licence-boundary review across four estimated INR 60,000 deliverables; INR 240,000 for separately accepted documentation, binding-build and contributor-support deliverables; up to INR 60,000 for uncovered incremental public-project CI/test/storage. External reviewers/support are not yet appointed and these are estimates, not recovered quotes. Maintainer and external tasks will not be double-charged. Usable credits reduce infrastructure costs; if fully covered this line and the request reduce accordingly. No overlapping costs will be charged to another funder.

Stewardship and sustainability: Abhishek Raj is the current sole human contributor and proposed lead; Xalen Technology Private Limited is the disclosed commercial sponsor and proposed recipient, subject to the foundation confirming the eligible structure. A documented build, traceable fixture corpus, public review findings and contributor entry tasks reduce dependence on one maintainer. Report actual independent reproductions and contributions when observed; no adopter counts or future contributors are guaranteed. Disclose other support during diligence. Public quarterly updates and agreed milestone check-ins make progress reviewable.
